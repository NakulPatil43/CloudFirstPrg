About Us

Codex Club is a community of passionate coders, developers, and tech enthusiasts. Our mission is to make students strong in their practical coding skills. While theory is often covered well in classrooms, many students struggle when it comes to actual coding. To bridge this gap, we provide hands-on learning by assigning real-world projects, coding challenges, and collaborative problem-solving activities. This way, students not only strengthen their technical knowledge but also gain confidence and experience in writing and executing code effectively.
